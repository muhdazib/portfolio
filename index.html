About Me

During my Diploma in Computer Science years, I have developed an interest in Information Technology especially software development. It was this interest that motivated me to learn multiple languages outside of my academic curriculum to perfect my software development skills.

I have completed my diploma journey with an internship at Ana Muslim Sdn. Bhd. My main project was to develop an e-commerce website using Content Management System (CMS) and PHP programming language. This internship journey has given me knowledge and experience that I am not able to learn in a classroom setting.

My interest in software development has led me to pursue a Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) with major in Big Data. For my final year project, I have developed a property analytics dashboard that analyses residential property values using Big Data technologies and data analytics. This project was a combination of my interest in software development and data analytics.

In my spare time, I enjoy exploring new projects and technologies, as well as deepening my knowledge in software development and data analytics through new projects and technologies. My hunger for knowledge and interest in software development and data analytics has become my number one drive in corporate settings.
